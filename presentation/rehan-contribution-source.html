1. Summary

Rehan is the project's test engineer. They built the Playwright test harness and its seed data, wrote the integration and ServiceNow test suites and their test-case catalogues, and recorded the case results used as evidence. In August they fixed the harness's JSON parsing. Commits dated August and September carry their ad hoc testing of the Approvals screen, Jest tests for the Lightning screens and a .env preflight check. The history places those commits in October (see "Dating" in section 4). In October they added the Apex test suites with mocked callouts (CAF-235). Later in October they moved into Salesforce build work and delivered six tickets of the October epic. CAF-218: fixed the blockers that stopped the callout log deploying. CAF-221: the CAF Viewer, Owner and Approver permission sets, with a role check on every action that writes to ServiceNow. CAF-222/226: the CAF Compliance Lightning app, tabs, icons and list views. CAF-224: the cafHome page with KPI tiles. CAF-229 and CAF-230: the task and request record pages. In Jira they own 55 CAF work items, mostly in the ETL and data quality epic. 35 are Done, and 9 of those were closed in October. Appendix A lists every Done ticket.

Dating

A commit cannot be made before the commits it is built on. Eight of Rehan's commits on main are dated earlier than commits they are built on, so the dates they carry cannot be when they were made. Seven are second copies of a change that is also on main with an October date. In every pair, the two copies have the same minutes and seconds and differ only in day and hour. For example, the cafHome change (CAF-224) is on main as 8e6b369, dated October, and as dac5fd0, dated September. dac5fd0 sits on top of a commit made in October.

Method and caveats

  • Source: git log main --no-merges at 125ac13, filtered by the email rehan.sadiq@reyantech.co.uk, which covers all three author names. The figures at the previous source were reproduced exactly before updating.
  • 7 of their 50 commits repeat the subject of another. Counts of "changes" use distinct subjects, dated by the earlier copy, with contradicted dates noted as described under "Dating". The Geethasri Chintha report uses the same method.
  • Commits measure activity, not effort or quality. Manual test execution, review and pairing are not captured.
  • Pull request data: gh pr view for #1–#41, read in October 2026, and #42–#47, read later in October. A pull request counts as theirs when every commit in it carries their email.
  • Jira data: issues assigned to account "Sadiq Rehan Mohammed", read in October 2026.
