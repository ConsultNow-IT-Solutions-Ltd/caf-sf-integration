1. Executive summary

The project gives application owners and approvers a Salesforce experience for CAF compliance while ServiceNow stays the system of record. The repository now holds a tested two-way integration: a Lightning application with a home page, compliance dashboard, approvals, reports, admin, and request and task record pages; a six-resource ServiceNow API; drift detection; durable callout logging; role-based permission sets; and a three-level test pack. The remaining five months take it through workflow completion, ETL testing and customer UAT to production readiness and handover by 31 March 2027.
