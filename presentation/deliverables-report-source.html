1. Executive summary

The project set out to give application owners and approvers a Salesforce experience for CAF compliance while ServiceNow stays the system of record. Over three delivered quarters it has gone from an empty repository to a working, tested two-way integration with four Salesforce screens, a six-resource ServiceNow API, drift detection, durable callout logging and a three-level test pack.
